Privacy policy

Homestill does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no ads, and it never sends any data to the developer or to any third party.

Homestill communicates only with the Home Assistant server address that you enter. When you sign in inside the app, your password is sent directly to your own server to sign in and is never stored on your device; alarm panel codes are also never stored.

To keep you signed in and open quickly, Homestill saves your server address and a Home Assistant refresh token on your device (in the Keychain on iPhone and in app-private storage on Android), along with a local cache of the last known state of your home. When you sign out, the app deletes the saved server address, refresh token, and local cache from your device and asks your Home Assistant server to revoke the refresh token.

Homestill is free, open-source software under the MIT license, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Home Assistant or Nabu Casa.

If you have questions about privacy, open an issue on the Homestill GitHub issues page.
