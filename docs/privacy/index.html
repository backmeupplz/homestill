Privacy policy

Homestill doesn't collect or share any personal data, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. What it keeps stays on your device.

The app connects only to the Home Assistant server whose address you enter. Your server address and a sign-in token that Home Assistant issues are stored on your device (the token in the Keychain on iPhone), and your password is never stored. A copy of your rooms and the last state of their devices is kept on your device so the app can show your home at once when it opens. Camera video and images come straight from your server.

Signing out asks your server to revoke the token and deletes it from your device, together with the saved copy of your home. Nothing is ever sent to the developer or to any third party.

Questions: open an issue.
