Homestill 1.0.2 lets you arrange the Home screen. Tap the pencil button on Home to open "Edit home", where you can reorder rooms and their devices, move a device to another room, and hide devices or whole rooms. Hidden ones stay dimmed in the editor to bring back, and "Reset Home layout" restores the default. The layout stays on your phone without writing to Home Assistant and clears on sign-out. New devices and rooms still appear on their own, and the Cameras tab is not customizable.
