First release of Homestill for Home Assistant. Control lights (brightness, color wheel, warmth), switches, fans, covers, locks, thermostats, water heaters, humidifiers, media players, vacuums, mowers, valves, sirens, remotes, alarm panels, scenes, scripts, and number or select settings by room, plus one-line sensor and weather tiles. Watch live HLS cameras (with 1-second still fallback), full-screen pinch to zoom, and ONVIF pan-and-tilt arrows, over one WebSocket with in-app 2FA login.
