Homestill 1.0.3 adds search and favorites. Search Home to filter devices by name or room as you type, and hidden devices show up in the results too, so they stay within reach. Tap the star next to a device in Edit home to add it to the new Favorites tab, where its tile works just as it does on Home, and use the pencil there to reorder or remove favorites. Favorites stay on your phone, survive Reset Home layout, and clear on sign-out.
