Homestill 1.0.1 fixes three visual issues. The app icon is redrawn smaller and bolder so it fits inside round and themed home-screen icons without being cropped. The app name on the sign-in screen is now readable in dark mode instead of showing dark text on a dark background. Colored light icons on device tiles now always stand out against their tile background in both light and dark mode, including blue bulbs in dark mode and yellow ones in light mode.
